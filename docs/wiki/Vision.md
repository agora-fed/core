# Vision & thesis

20 years of civic-tech failed because **participation had no consequence**. Decidim had honest
intent and solid engineering, yet officials could ignore proposals with zero cost; proposals drowned
in noise; citizens never saw the platform; and there was no persistent accountability artifact.

This platform is **not neutral and says so**: it is an accountability instrument. The product is the
**consequence loop** — propose, cluster into real consensus, vote, notify the official, run a public
SLA clock, and record answered/acted/**ignored** on a permanent public scorecard.
