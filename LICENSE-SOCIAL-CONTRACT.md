# Social Contract Clause

This software is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later** (see `LICENSE`). In the spirit of
Decidim's democratic guarantees, deployments that present this platform as an instrument of
**public participation** additionally commit to:

1. **Transparency of code and rules.** The running version's source and its moderation/consensus
   rules are publicly inspectable. Auditability is a right of the participating public.
2. **No silent censorship.** Moderation decisions are logged and appealable; no opaque automated
   classifier removes civic speech without an auditable record (PLAN.md principle 11).
3. **Data sovereignty & minimization.** Citizen data stays under sovereign control; individual
   vote linkage is minimized and never exposed to officials.
4. **Accountability is preserved.** The consequence/scorecard records are public and may not be
   silently altered or deleted to protect an official.
5. **Right to fork.** Communities may self-host and federate; the platform must not create a single
   point of takedown or capture.

This clause expresses intent and governance commitments; it does not restrict the freedoms granted
by the AGPL. Where in tension, the AGPL controls. CHARRUA v1.2 is under evaluation for
sovereign-only components (PLAN.md §10).
